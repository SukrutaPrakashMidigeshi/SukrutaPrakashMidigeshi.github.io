About Me

I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon. Before this, I was a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India (2024–2026), where I worked with Gaurav Sinha on bandit algorithms with limited adaptivity and on reasoning in large language models.

I received my B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar in 2024. There I worked on approximating neural tangent kernels with Prof. Anirban Dasgupta, and I also collaborate with Prof. Siddharth Barman on algorithmic game theory. Earlier, I interned at D. E. Shaw, eLitmus and goGlocal.

Research Interests

  • Bandits & Online Learning: algorithms with provable regret guarantees, especially under limited adaptivity (batched and rarely-switching policies)
  • Algorithms & Algorithmic Game Theory: optimization and learning from weak feedback such as comparison oracles
  • Reasoning in LLMs: which algorithmic tasks language models can express and solve, planning-guided retrieval, and principled benchmarks
