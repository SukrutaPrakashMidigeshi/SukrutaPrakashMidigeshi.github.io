## About Me

I am a Research Fellow at [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/) under [Dr. Gaurav Sinha](https://sinhagaurav.github.io/), working on Reinforcement Learning and Reasoning.

My first line of work focuses on the theoretical and practical implementation of bandit algorithms across various settings, including logistic, GLM, and multinomial logistic reward models. I am currently working on developing more practical algorithms for GLM bandits in the stochastic contextual batched setting.

My second line of work aims to improve the retrieval and reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs). In particular, I am building a theoretical framework for understanding the expressivity and solvability of different algorithmic tasks by LLMs.

Prior to this, I completed my undergraduate studies at [IIT Gandhinagar](https://iitgn.ac.in) with an Honours in Computer Science and Engineering. During my B.Tech, I worked on Fair Division, NTK kernels, and Group Theory. I also interned at [D. E. Shaw](https://www.deshawindia.com/), eLitmus and goGlocal working on RAG-systems and ML evaluation frameworks.
