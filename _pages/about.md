I am an Applied Scientist at Amazon. Before this, I was a Research Fellow at [Microsoft Research India](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/) (2024–2026), where I worked with [Dr. Gaurav Sinha](https://sinhagaurav.github.io/).

My research is centered on **theory**: the design and analysis of algorithms for sequential decision-making and learning.

- **Bandits and online learning.** I design algorithms with provable regret guarantees for logistic, GLM and multinomial-logistic reward models, with a focus on *limited adaptivity*: achieving near-optimal regret while updating the policy only a few times (batched and rarely-switching settings).
- **Algorithms and algorithmic game theory.** I study learning and optimization from weak feedback, such as recovering the objective of packing linear programs and fractional knapsack from comparison oracles, joint work with [Prof. Siddharth Barman](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~barman/).
- **Reasoning in LLMs.** I am also interested in what algorithmic tasks language models can express and solve. This has led to work on reachability-style reasoning puzzles, planning-guided retrieval (PlanRAG), and a formal definition and benchmark for deep research (LiveDRBench).

I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at [IIT Gandhinagar](https://iitgn.ac.in) (CGPA 9.07/10). There I worked on fair division, approximating neural tangent kernels with locality-sensitive hashing, and group theory. I also interned at [D. E. Shaw](https://www.deshawindia.com/), eLitmus and goGlocal, working on ML evaluation, retrieval and NLP systems.
