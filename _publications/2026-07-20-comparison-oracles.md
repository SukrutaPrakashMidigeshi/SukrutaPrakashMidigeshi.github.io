We study how to recover the objective of a packing linear program when the algorithm only has comparison information about optimal solutions under varying constraints. We focus on fractional knapsack, which captures monopoly pricing where a seller infers a buyer's unknown valuations for divisible items. Authors are listed in alphabetical order, as is customary in theoretical computer science.
