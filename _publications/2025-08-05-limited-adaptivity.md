Multinomial logistic bandits model decisions with many possible outcomes. Motivated by practical deployments, we design algorithms that are allowed only a limited number of policy updates: B-MNL-CB for the batched setting and RS-MNL for the rarely-switching setting. Both achieve near-optimal regret while remaining computationally efficient. Paper also available in the [Reinforcement Learning Journal](https://rlj.cs.umass.edu/2025/papers/Paper383.html).
