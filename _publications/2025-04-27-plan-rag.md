Plan-RAG changes the retrieve-then-reason paradigm of standard RAG to plan-then-retrieve. It formulates a reasoning plan as a directed acyclic graph that decomposes a query into interrelated atomic sub-queries, which can be answered in parallel. It outperforms recent methods such as Self-RAG on standard multi-hop reasoning benchmarks at comparable cost. OpenReview: [link](https://openreview.net/forum?id=cUuOKnjVQJ).
