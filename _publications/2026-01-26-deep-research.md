We argue that the defining feature of deep research is not long report-style output but high fan-out over concepts during search: broad, reasoning-intensive exploration. We formalize the task and introduce LiveDRBench, a benchmark of 100 challenging tasks over scientific topics and public-interest events, and evaluate state-of-the-art systems on it. Code and data: [github.com/microsoft/livedrbench](https://github.com/microsoft/livedrbench).
