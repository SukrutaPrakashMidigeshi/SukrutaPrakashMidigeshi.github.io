We study contextual slate bandits where the reward of a slate follows a generalized linear model, under two limited-adaptivity settings: batched and rarely-switching. Our algorithms (B-SlateGLinCB and RS-SlateGLinCB) achieve near-optimal regret with few policy updates, and stay competitive with fully adaptive baselines. We also apply the framework to example selection for prompt tuning.
